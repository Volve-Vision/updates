# Security

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you believe you have found a security issue in any Volve Vision product, please report it
privately to **support@volve.vision** with `SECURITY` in the subject, rather than opening a public issue.

Useful things to include:

- What you found and what it lets someone do.
- The steps to reproduce it.
- The product and surface involved, and roughly when you observed it.

## What happens next

- We aim to acknowledge a report within **3 working days**.
- We will tell you whether we can reproduce it, and give you a sense of timing.
- We will let you know when the fix is live.
- With your permission, we are glad to credit you once the issue is resolved.

## Please do not

- Access, modify or delete data that is not yours.
- Degrade the service for other people, including load testing and denial of service.
- Use social engineering, phishing or physical access against our people or partners.
- Publish details of an unfixed issue.

Reports made in good faith under these terms are welcome, and we will not pursue action over them.

## Scope

This repository holds published updates only. It contains no source code, so issues here would be
content errors rather than vulnerabilities. Report those as normal issues.
