# 2022 to 2023

**In one line:** the first generation, which proved the idea and established the company.

---

## What existed

Volve Vision was founded in May 2022. The first generation consisted of a mobile application and
the service behind it, followed by a second attempt at the app in late 2022 that carried into 2023.

That generation is no longer in service. Everything running today was rebuilt from scratch starting
in December 2025, and shares no code with it.

---

## What it proved

- People will watch live cameras from places they have never been, for longer than you expect.
- The hard part is not showing a stream. It is keeping thousands of them alive, described, findable
  and worth returning to.
- Those problems are worth a platform rather than an app.

Every one of those lessons shaped what was built in 2026.

---

## Why it is in the record

A company that presents itself as starting in 2026 is telling half a story. The first generation is
where the idea was tested and the mistakes were made that the current platform was designed to
avoid.
