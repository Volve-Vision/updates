# June 2026

*Platform release `2026.06`.*

**In one line:** the platform got a voice - editorial, content production in three languages, and
the tooling to keep it honest.

---

## Highlights

**A news section with a real newsroom behind it.**
Categories, topics, authors, scheduling, multi-language articles and a review step. Articles link
to the cameras they are about, which is the point: an article about a storm is more useful next to
a camera pointing at it.

**Content production at catalogue scale.**
Nearly 5,000 cameras and 1,000 cities each need their own description in each language. June is when
that stopped being manual: a production pipeline with per-language readiness indicators, a review
step, and a rule that nothing half-finished gets published.

**Comment moderation that does not need a moderator on duty.**
Abusive comments are hidden automatically in all supported languages, including attempts to disguise
them. Anything uncertain waits for a human.

---

## By product

### Web platform
- `Added` The news section: categories, topics, authors, multi-language articles, related cameras.
- `Added` Comment moderation, applied across all languages.
- `Added` Location pages for cities and regions that previously had no page of their own.
- `Changed` How camera pages describe themselves to search engines, in every language.
- `Fixed` Breadcrumb trails that could point at pages which did not exist.

### Operations console
- `Added` The editorial hub: everything needed to run the news section.
- `Added` Bulk content production with per-language readiness indicators.
- `Added` Image selection tooling for editorial work.

### Content and catalogue
- `Added` A production pipeline covering descriptions, translations and metadata.
- `Added` Automatic submission of new pages to search engines as they are published.
- `Changed` Country and region naming unified across the catalogue.

### Platform, behind the scenes
- `Security` Additional browser-level protections across public surfaces, introduced in a
  monitoring mode first so that nothing broke for visitors before it was switched on.
- `Reliability` Protection against a slow upstream source dragging down unrelated pages.
