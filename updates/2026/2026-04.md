# April 2026

*Platform release `2026.04`.*

**In one line:** the new website went live and replaced the previous generation entirely.

---

## Highlights

**A new volvevision.com.**
Catalogue, maps, search and a page for every camera, city and country. Rebuilt from scratch on a
foundation that could carry the products that followed rather than patched onto what existed before.

**Cameras that check themselves.**
The first automated health checks: a camera that stops working is detected rather than waiting for
someone to notice.

**A pipeline for new cameras.**
Bringing in a new source, preparing its content and publishing it became a defined process instead
of a manual one.

- `Added` Multi-language structure from day one rather than retrofitted.

### Platform, behind the scenes
