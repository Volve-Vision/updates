# July 2026

*Platform release `2026.07`. Mobile app 1.0.*

**In one line:** the mobile apps went from nothing to a complete product in a single month.

---

## Highlights

**A real app, not a wrapped website.**
Catalogue, player, map, search, favourites, news and profile, in three languages, with light and
dark themes and the device language detected automatically. Home-screen widgets on both platforms.
Voice shortcuts, quick actions, picture-in-picture and saved cameras that work offline.
Full detail: [mobile apps](../../products/mobile-apps.md).

**Sign in with Apple, and account deletion that actually deletes.**
Both were built properly rather than to the minimum the stores accept, including revoking the sign-in
grant when an account is deleted.

**Live presence on camera pages.**
Viewer counts and live reactions, so an empty-looking stream shows that other people are watching
it too.

---

## By product

### Mobile apps (1.0)
- `Added` The app: catalogue, camera player, map, search, favourites, news, profile.
- `Added` Light, dark and system themes; English, Russian and Spanish with device autodetect.
- `Added` Sign in with Apple on iOS and Google sign-in; in-app account deletion.
- `Added` Home-screen widgets and picture-in-picture on both platforms; quick actions, voice
  shortcuts and spotlight search on iOS.
- `Added` Offline access to saved cameras; cameras near you, using device location.
- `Added` Camera submission and owner analytics, matching the website.
- `Added` A haptics layer with a switch for people who do not want it.
- `Added` Accessibility support for large text sizes throughout.
- `Performance` Long lists rebuilt on a faster foundation.

### Web platform
- `Performance` Home and camera pages: less layout movement while loading, faster first paint.
- `Changed` Advertising layout no longer shifts content while a page settles.
- `Added` First-party promotional slots that behave predictably next to third-party ones.

### Platform, behind the scenes
- `Reliability` Backup scheduling moved off a contended hour and made to catch up after a restart.
- `Added` Live presence and reactions, rate-limited, on a fixed set of reactions.
- `Added` Camera reporting, so a viewer can flag a problem with a specific feed.
