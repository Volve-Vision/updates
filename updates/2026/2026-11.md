# November 2026

*Platform release `2026.11`, in progress, last updated 2026-11-04. iOS app 2.1 is on the App Store; iOS 2.1.1 is in
App Review. Google Play is still at 1.1. The new VolveMenu phone screens listed below are built and not yet in the
stores; the other VolveMenu items are live in the web workspace.*

**In one line:** the iOS app gained its first paid option and a new way to watch, a venue can now run its own
website from VolveMenu, and more than one person can own a venue.

---

## Highlights

**Volve Vision 2.1 on the App Store.**
The first paid option in the app is Volve Pro, an optional subscription that removes the ads before cameras; the
rest of the app stays available without it. Every camera now has a full screen button, and a camera swiped down
shrinks into a bar above the tabs while the viewer keeps browsing. A phone notification tells people when someone
replies to their comment. All of this is on iOS only for now.

**A venue's website, run from VolveMenu.**
An owner now changes what their website shows in one place: a notice with a last day, days that differ from the
usual week, the lunch price, the rating, guests' words and photos by place. A holiday or a short day is announced
on the site a day ahead in the visitor's language, changes the open status and is passed on to search engines.
Page titles, headings and addresses stay out of reach on purpose, because search ranks them.

**What the website brings back.**
Requests left on the site appear in the workspace and reach the team as a phone notification on iOS. The owner also
sees the site's views, calls, directions and requests for the last 30 days, and whether the site picked up the last
change.

**More than one owner.**
A venue can have several owners with the same rights. Only the account the venue belongs to can delete it, and
nobody can remove that account.

## By product

### Mobile apps
- `Added` Volve Pro, an optional subscription without ads before cameras. On iOS, from version 2.1.
- `Added` A full screen button for every camera. On iOS, from version 2.1.
- `Added` A phone notification when someone replies to a comment, with a switch in settings. On iOS, from version 2.1.
- `Changed` A camera swiped down shrinks into a bar above the tabs, in one motion with its page. On iOS, from version 2.1.
- `Fixed` Country borders and labels on the map and the planet. In iOS 2.1.1, submitted to App Review and not yet
  available.
- Android: full screen, the bar and reply notifications are in internal testing, and Volve Pro is not switched on
  there. The public Google Play version is still 1.1.

### Web platform
- `Fixed` A wrong regional label on the map.

### VolveMenu
- `Added` A "Site" section in the web workspace: notice, special days, price, rating, quotes, photos by place,
  requests and counters. Live.
- `Added` Co-owners, invited by link or chosen in the team list. Live in the web workspace.
- `Added` API keys per venue, so a venue's own systems can read and change its menu without reaching anything else.
- `Added` The same section and a venue details screen in the phone app, built and not yet in the stores.
- `Changed` A photo chosen for a website must belong to the venue and be wide enough for its place, so a small
  snapshot cannot blur a large area.
- `Reliability` A website connected to VolveMenu keeps showing the last good menu if the service does not answer.
