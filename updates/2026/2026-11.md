# November 2026

*Platform release `2026.11`, in progress, last updated 2026-11-04. The new VolveMenu phone screens listed below
are built and not yet in the stores; everything else is live in the web workspace.*

**In one line:** a venue can now run its own website from VolveMenu, and more than one person can own a venue.

---

## Highlights

**A venue's website, run from VolveMenu.**
An owner now changes what their website shows in one place: a notice with a last day, days that differ from the
usual week, the lunch price, the rating, guests' words and photos by place. A holiday or a short day is announced
on the site a day ahead in the visitor's language, changes the open status and is passed on to search engines.
Page titles, headings and addresses stay out of reach on purpose, because search ranks them.

**What the website brings back.**
Requests left on the site appear in the workspace and reach the team as a phone notification on iOS. The owner also
sees the site's views, calls, directions and requests for the last 30 days, and whether the site picked up the last
change.

**More than one owner.**
A venue can have several owners with the same rights. Only the account the venue belongs to can delete it, and
nobody can remove that account.

## By product

### VolveMenu
- `Added` A "Site" section in the web workspace: notice, special days, price, rating, quotes, photos by place,
  requests and counters. Live.
- `Added` Co-owners, invited by link or chosen in the team list. Live in the web workspace.
- `Added` API keys per venue, so a venue's own systems can read and change its menu without reaching anything else.
- `Added` The same section and a venue details screen in the phone app, built and not yet in the stores.
- `Changed` A photo chosen for a website must belong to the venue and be wide enough for its place, so a small
  snapshot cannot blur a large area.
- `Reliability` A website connected to VolveMenu keeps showing the last good menu if the service does not answer.
