# May 2026

*Platform release `2026.05`.*

**In one line:** the platform became a business - accounts, subscriptions, payments and a way for
camera owners to earn.

---

## Highlights

**Accounts, properly.**
Sign-in that survives a closed laptop, sessions tied to devices, protection against automated
sign-up abuse, and a session list a person can actually understand. This is the layer everything
else was later built on, including the security centre that shipped in September.

**Subscriptions and payments.**
Trials, upgrades with correct proration, receipts, yearly plans and per-camera subscriptions.
Payment handling was built to be idempotent from the start, so a retried payment can never be
charged or fulfilled twice.

**Camera owners can earn.**
A full monetisation layer: promoted placement for cameras, advertising campaigns with multiple
creatives, moderation before anything goes live, and reporting for both the owner and the operator.

**An operations console instead of manual work.**
Moderation, catalogue management, campaign review, commercial views and content pipelines moved into
one internal tool with role-based access.

## By product

- `Added` Accounts: durable sessions, device awareness, sign-up protection.

### Operations console
- `Added` The console itself, with role-based access.
- `Added` Moderation queues for cameras, comments and campaigns.
- `Added` Commercial and usage views.
- `Added` Catalogue intake tooling for new camera sources.

### Platform, behind the scenes
- `Added` Automated daily backups with a health check that blocks releases if backups are stale.
- `Added` Automated release pipelines for every surface, with a manual approval gate.
- `Reliability` Protection against slow or failing upstream camera sources.
- `Performance` Caching and database work across the busiest paths.
- `Security` Hardening across account handling, uploads and administrative access.
