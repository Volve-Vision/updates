# February 2026

*Platform release `2026.02`. The first month of the rebuild.*

**In one line:** the foundation - the service layer the current platform still runs on.

---

## Highlights

**Starting over rather than patching.**
The decision that shaped 2026: rather than extending the 2022 generation, the platform was rebuilt.
February is where that began, with the service layer that everything since has been built on.

**Payments and email from the start.**
Payment handling and transactional email were part of the foundation rather than bolted on later,
which is why the subscription work in May took weeks instead of months.
