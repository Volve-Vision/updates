# August 2026

*Platform release `2026.08`. Mobile app 1.1. VolveMenu 1.0.*

**In one line:** a new product shipped end to end in four weeks, and German became a real language
rather than a translation.

---

## Highlights

**VolveMenu, from nothing to live.**
Digital menu boards for venues: build the menu on a phone, pair a screen with a short code, and it
works. Screens keep working without internet and catch up when it returns. Media uploaded from a
phone is prepared automatically for a large screen. Every new account gets its own copy of finished
demo menus, so the product is never empty on first open. Full detail: [VolveMenu](../../products/volvemenu.md).

**German end to end.**
Not a translation pass: the site, the apps, email, notifications, error messages and the SEO layer
all speak German. That is the difference between a language a visitor can use and one they bounce
off. It took work in every part of the system, and most of it is invisible.

**Offline cameras stopped being deleted from the record.**
A camera that goes dark keeps its page and its history for a defined window rather than vanishing.
Viewers stop hitting dead ends, and pages that took months to earn their standing are not thrown away.

## By product

### Web platform
- `Added` German as a fourth full language across every page type.
- `Added` An install banner for phone visitors, pointing at the native apps.
- `Changed` Offline cameras keep their page for a defined window, marked clearly as offline.
- `Changed` A page is only offered to search engines in a language it actually has content in.
- `Fixed` Pages renamed in the past keep their canonical identity instead of competing with themselves.

### Mobile apps (1.1)
- `Added` German localisation.
- `Added` A rebuilt map: category filters, camera list at the bottom, live preview markers,
  country highlighting and directions to any camera.
- `Added` Full-screen landscape multi-camera view.
- `Performance` Long camera grids scroll smoothly on older devices.

### VolveMenu (1.0)
- `Added` Everything: venues, menus, screen pairing, offline delivery, real-time updates,
  media handling, push notifications on iOS, snapshots, share links and demo content.

### Content and catalogue
- `Added` German content production alongside the existing three languages.
- `Added` Localised frequently-asked-questions on country pages.
- `Changed` Automatic daily submission of new cameras to search engines.
- `Fixed` Descriptions that had been cut mid-sentence.
- `Fixed` City pages whose parent country was never set, which had left them orphaned.
