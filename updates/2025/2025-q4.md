# Q4 2025

**In one line:** the decision to rebuild, and the first piece of it.

---

## What happened

Volve Vision had been running since 2022 on its first generation of apps. By late 2025 it was clear
that the direction the company wanted to go - a real catalogue at global scale, native apps, and
products built on top of the camera platform - was not reachable from where that code stood.

December 2025 is where the rebuild starts. The first artefact is the company landing page; the
platform work begins in February 2026 and has been continuous since.

- `Added` The company landing page.
- `Changed` The direction: rebuild rather than extend.

---

## Why this month is in the record

Because a public history that starts at the first launch is a marketing timeline, not a record.
The decision to throw away working code and start again is the most consequential one in this
repository, and it belongs here.
