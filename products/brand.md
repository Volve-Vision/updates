# Brand and 3D

The identity across everything: the site, the apps, the screens in venues, the store listings and
the printed material.

---

## The mark

Volve Vision is a camera company, so the brand leans on what a camera sees: clear light blues
against near-black, high contrast, no decoration that gets in the way of a live image. The palette
holds up in both light and dark interfaces because both are used, everywhere.

## The mascot

A small robot appears across the products: in the apps where a screen has nothing to show yet, in
onboarding, and in marketing material. It exists because a catalogue of 5,000 live cameras is a
serious thing that does not need to feel cold.

In September 2026 the mascot became a real 3D asset rather than a flat illustration, which means it
can be posed and animated for any surface instead of being redrawn each time.
