# Operations console

Internal only. No public address, and none will be published.

Everything the public sees is curated by someone. The console is where that happens: moderation,
publishing, content production and quality dashboards, in one place for the small team that runs
the platform.

It is listed here because a meaningful share of the engineering effort in 2026 went into it, and a
record that left it out would misrepresent how the work was actually spent.

---

## What it covers

- **Moderation.** Submitted cameras, comments and user reports, reviewed before anything is public.
- **Catalogue management.** Adding, editing, retiring and re-checking cameras, in bulk when needed.
- **Content production.** Preparing and publishing descriptions and editorial content across four
  languages, with a review step before anything goes live.
- **Quality dashboards.** Which cameras are healthy, which pages are missing content, what search
  engines are finding and what they are not.
- **Customer administration.** Subscriptions, plans and support actions.
- **Announcements.** Branded email to people who asked to receive it.
