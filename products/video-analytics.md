# Video analytics

Turning camera feeds into numbers a business can act on. **In development. Not available to
customers yet, and not sold as if it were.**

---

## The idea

A venue, a municipality or a retailer usually already has cameras. What they do not have is a
straightforward answer to questions like how many people came in this morning, how long the queue
was at noon, or whether the car park filled up before lunch.

Volve Vision already carries live feeds at scale and knows how to keep them healthy. The analytics
layer is what sits on top of that: counting and detection that runs against a feed the customer
already owns, producing a small number of honest, useful numbers.

## Why it matters to the rest of the platform

It is the first product where the customer brings the camera and pays for what we do with it,
rather than watching cameras we carry. That is a different business shape from everything else on
the platform, and it is the reason the work is being done.
