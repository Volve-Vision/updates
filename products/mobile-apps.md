# Mobile apps

Native iOS and Android clients for the camera catalogue. Development started in July 2026. Both
apps are publicly available: the iOS app has been on the App Store since 29 July 2026 and the
Android app on Google Play since 22 July 2026. The published iOS version is **2.1**, released on
4 November 2026, and 2.1.1 is in App Review. The published Android version is **2.1.1**, released
on 4 November 2026.

---

## What they do

- The full catalogue on a phone: browse, search, filter, map.
- Watch a live camera, save it, share it, snapshot it.
- Cameras near you, and a map that behaves like a map should.
- Home-screen widgets on both platforms; quick actions and voice shortcuts on iOS.
- Multi-camera view for watching several feeds at once.
- Offline access to saved cameras.
- Account, notifications and security settings shared with the website.
- 36 interface languages, with the device language detected automatically.
- Volve Pro on iOS: an optional subscription without ads before cameras.

## Release history

### 2.1.1 - Android, 4 November 2026; iOS, in App Review

On Android this is the first public update since 1.1. It carries the items of 1.2, 2.0 and 2.1 below,
except Volve Pro, iPad layouts and sign-in with Apple. On iOS it was submitted on 4 November 2026 and is
not yet available.

- **Fixed** country borders and labels on the map and the planet.

### 2.1 - iOS, 4 November 2026

- **Added** Volve Pro, an optional subscription that removes the ads before cameras.
- **Added** a full screen button for every camera.
- **Added** a phone notification when someone replies to a comment, with a switch in settings.
- **Changed** a camera swiped down now shrinks into a bar above the tabs while you browse.

### 2.0 - iOS, live by 2 November 2026

- **Added** Planet now: aurora forecast, waves, weather, travel advice and live cloud cover on one map.
- **Added** a new Home with a day and night band, Golden hour now, Surprise me and Continue watching.
- **Added** conditions on camera pages: weather, waves, aurora after dark and travel advice.
- **Added** live event boards with up to four streams, and AI moments from cameras worldwide.
- **Added** 36 languages, iPad layouts, and sign-in with Apple, Google or email.

### 1.2 - built September 2026, never released on its own

This release stayed in internal testing and was never submitted to a store. Its items reached iOS
users through version 2.0 and Android users through version 2.1.1.

- **Added** the account security centre: sessions across all devices, recent security events,
  and remote sign-out.
- **Changed** sign-in with Apple to match Apple's own presentation rules exactly.
- **Fixed** the map moving the camera to a point hidden behind the bottom sheet.
- **Changed** the mascot's appearance in empty states so it appears where it has something to say
  rather than everywhere.

### 1.1 - August 2026

- **Added** German, bringing the app to four languages.
- **Added** a redesigned map: category filters, a camera list at the bottom, live preview markers,
  and directions to any camera.
- **Added** full-screen landscape multi-camera view.
- **Performance** work on long camera grids, which now scroll smoothly on older devices.

### 1.0 - July 2026

The first release. Built from nothing in a single month:

- **Added** the core app: catalogue, camera player, map, search, favourites, news and profile.
- **Added** light, dark and system themes; English, Russian and Spanish.
- **Added** sign in with Apple on iOS and Google sign-in, plus in-app account deletion.
- **Added** live viewer counts and live reactions on camera pages.
- **Added** home-screen widgets and picture-in-picture on both platforms, and iOS quick actions,
  voice shortcuts and spotlight search for saved cameras.
- **Added** offline caching of saved cameras.
- **Added** "cameras near me" using device location.
- **Added** camera submission and owner analytics, matching what the website offers.
- **Added** a considered haptics layer, with a switch for people who do not want it.
- **Added** accessibility support for large text sizes throughout.
